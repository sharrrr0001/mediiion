# Mediaura.ai — Doctor quick start (pilot)

**What it does:** before the visit, your patient talks to **Aura**, our assistant, by chat or voice, in Hindi, English or Hinglish. Aura asks your clinic's questions and organises the patient's own words into a **Pre-Visit Summary**. You read it in 30 seconds, write the Assessment and Plan, and finalise. Aura never diagnoses or prescribes.

## Set up (10 minutes)
1. Open the site → **I'm a doctor → Create doctor account** → sign in with your email code.
2. Enter your registration number and medical council → upload your **registration certificate** and **government ID**.
3. Add your **clinic, timings, slot length and fees**.
4. Log in again and set up **2-step verification** (Google Authenticator or similar).
5. Our team verifies you, usually within 48 hours. Your profile then goes live.
6. **Plan & billing** → enter your pilot code **PILOT2M** (2 months of Pro free).

## Every clinic day
- **Today's queue** (`/d/today`): patients with a green **Summary ready** tag already did their pre-visit.
- **Walk-in or no booking?** Press **Scan QR** and scan the patient's Mediaura ID. The patient taps **Allow** on their phone, and their records open for 24 hours.
- Open a patient → read the summary (every line shows the patient's own words) → write **Assessment** and **Plan**, add medicines → **Review & finalise**. Only then does it enter the patient's record book.
- Rate the summary 👍/👎. It helps us improve.

## Your receptionist
**Staff → Invite staff** (by email). They sign in at `/d/login` and use the **Clinic desk**: today's visits, walk-in check-in, and the **waiting-room QR** to print. Receptionists never see health information.

## Waiting-room QR
**Staff → Waiting-room QR → Print.** Put it at reception: *"Doctor se pehle 5 minute AI se baat karein."* Patients scan it, talk to Aura, and the summary reaches you before they walk in.

## Good to know
- Every summary is labelled **"AI-generated draft. Not a diagnosis or prescription. Treating doctor must review."**
- If a patient mentions an emergency, Aura shows a fixed safety message ("Call 112"). You see a neutral marker in the queue.
- Patients control access and can stop it any time; every view is logged.
- Help: Settings → Support, or reply to any Mediaura email.
